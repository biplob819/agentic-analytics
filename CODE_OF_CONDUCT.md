# Code of conduct

agentic-analytics is a place to build careful, honest evaluation tooling together. We want
everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space, to be
treated with respect, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be precise and honest, especially about results: say what the evidence shows and what it does
  not, and keep intervals next to every figure.
- Welcome newcomers and questions; explain context rather than dismissing.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on scope gracefully, and disagree openly but civilly.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults or personal attacks; discriminatory jokes or language; sexualised language
or imagery; publishing someone's private information; sustained disruption; and encouraging any
of these.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately through the repository's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/biplob819/agentic-analytics/security/advisories/new)
form (choose a clear title such as "conduct report"). If that form is not available to you, or
the report is not about security, [open a public issue](https://github.com/biplob819/agentic-analytics/issues/new) titled
**"Conduct contact request"** with no details, names or links: the maintainer replies on the
issue with a private channel for the report, and the issue is closed without discussing it in
public. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions, and warn, suspend or ban participants, as the situation warrants.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
