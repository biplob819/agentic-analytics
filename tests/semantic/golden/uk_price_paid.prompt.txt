SEMANTIC DESCRIPTIONS
Generated from the uk_price_paid semantic model: what each column means, its synonyms and sample values.

Overview: Residential property sales in England and Wales from HM Land Registry's Price Paid Data, one row per sale, from 1 January 1995 onwards. Prices are in pounds sterling (GBP). Place names (town, district, county, locality, street) are stored in uppercase, so compare them with uppercase text such as 'LONDON'. Missing text values are empty strings, not NULL. Every question comes with an as-of date, the last day of the month before the latest month in the data: treat it as today and do not count sales after it. Sales appear only after they are registered, weeks to months after completion, so the last few months before the as-of date are under-counted and keep filling in with later releases. Repossessions and buy-to-let sales (category B) are included and cannot be told apart from standard sales.
